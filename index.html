About

I'm a self-taught developer from Venezuela, based in Colombia. I came to code through drawing and design — I like creating an interface first and then figuring out how to bring it to life.

I care about the parts people feel but rarely notice: spacing that breathes, states that make sense, buttons that respond the way you expect. I build small tools I actually use, because that's the fastest way to find out what's missing.

I'm working my way toward full-stack, and eventually games — the one place where interface and play are the same thing.
